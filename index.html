VolleyScore Help

Version 1.0.0

  • Score: Tap the upper half of HOME or AWAY to add a point. Tap the lower half to subtract one. Scores cannot go below 00.
  • Win: Reach at least 25 points with a two-point lead. After a win, additions stop, but corrections can reopen the set.
  • Set timer: Starts with the first point, stops at a win, and resumes if a correction removes the win.
  • Countdown: Tap 2:00 to start a two-minute countdown before minute :58. It also starts automatically at :58 each hour, counting down to the next hour. A countdown already running keeps its end time. Active countdowns cannot restart; expiration shows TIMES UP without sound.
  • New set: Immediately clears scores and both timers. During minutes :58 and :59, it cancels that hour's automatic countdown.
  • Offline: No internet is needed. The clock uses local device time. Reloading or closing the page loses scores and timers.

Help does not pause either timer. Use Tab to focus controls and Enter or Space to activate them. Close this popup with Close or Escape.
